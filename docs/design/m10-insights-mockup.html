          Keep it fresh

          now
          Spotify takes from a few days up to 30 to prepare an Extended streaming history. Requesting one is done on spotify.com; Runout can't do it for you.
          Collection, Your take and Year in review stay as they areYear in review gains the Report tab's year view (vs last year, top new, records set that year).
          Left out, and why
          BPM, energy, mood, danceabilitySpotify removed audio features in 2024. Runout can't analyse the audio itself (protected playback, client files off limits). AcousticBrainz stopped in 2022 and covers little recent music.
          "How mainstream" you areNeeds other listeners' counts; Spotify's popularity field is removed and the API is barred from insights anyway.
          Genres, decades, artist countries without the open databaseNo source in Spotify's export. With your OK, the Genres & decades tab covers them.
          Where discoveries came from (playlist, radio, search)The export doesn't say. Replaced by "How you started tracks".
          Free-text "ask anything" boxNeeds an AI model: no network allowed, no remote processing. Replaced by Explore and ready-made questions.
          Travel / countries you listened fromDropped on import by design (location data, data minimisation).
          Podcasts and audiobooksOut of scope (music only); dropped on import.
          Album played start to finish, for every albumNeeds track order. Only albums whose track list you've added in Runout can be checked.
          Comparisons with other peopleRunout has only your data. That's the point.
